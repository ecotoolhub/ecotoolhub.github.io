Understanding the 30:1 Composting Ratio

Aerobic composting relies on microbes breaking down organic matter. Maintaining a 30:1 carbon-to-nitrogen ratio speeds up decomposition while eliminating foul odors in stainless steel countertop bins and outdoor tumblers.
