🌫️ Lahore AQI: What Today's Number Means

Lahore regularly ranks among the world's most polluted cities, especially during the winter smog season (November-February) when the AQI often crosses 200 and sometimes 300. In summer, rain and winds usually bring it down to the moderate range.

What each AQI band means for you in Lahore

0–50 · Good

Enjoy outdoor activities normally. Lahore rarely sees this in winter, but monsoon-season days can get here.

51–100 · Moderate

Acceptable for most people. Sensitive groups (asthma, elderly, young children) should watch for symptoms on longer outings.

101–150 · Unhealthy for sensitive groups

Children, elderly, pregnant women, and anyone with heart/lung conditions should shorten or move outdoor exertion indoors.

151–200 · Unhealthy

Everyone may feel effects. Keep windows closed, run a HEPA purifier, wear an N95 outdoors, skip the morning jog.

201–300 · Very unhealthy

Health alert conditions. Avoid all outdoor exertion; keep children and elderly indoors as much as possible.

300+ · Hazardous

Emergency conditions. Stay indoors, seal windows, run purifiers on high. Lahore hits this during peak smog episodes.

LOCAL CONTEXT

Why Lahore's air behaves this way

The winter smog comes from a mix of vehicle exhaust, brick kilns, crop burning in surrounding Punjab, and dust — trapped by cold air in a seasonal inversion. Mornings are usually worst; afternoons improve slightly as the sun warms the air.

Lahore Air Quality FAQs

Is Lahore's air quality dangerous?

During smog season, yes. AQI readings above 200 (very unhealthy) are common in winter, and spikes above 300 (hazardous) happen. Even healthy adults should limit outdoor exertion on such days; children, the elderly, and asthma patients should stay indoors.

When is Lahore's smog season?

Typically November through February. October can bring early episodes, and March usually clears as temperatures rise and winds pick up.

Should I wear a mask in Lahore?

On days when AQI crosses 150, an N95/FFP2 mask outdoors makes a real difference. Ordinary cloth or surgical masks do almost nothing against PM2.5 — the tiny particles driving Lahore's smog.

What AQI level is safe for jogging in Lahore?

Under 100 is fine for most people. Between 100–150, sensitive people should move workouts indoors. Above 150, everyone should skip outdoor exercise — running multiplies the air you breathe 10–20x.
