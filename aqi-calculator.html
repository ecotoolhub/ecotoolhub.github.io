Understanding Air Quality Index (AQI) and Health Impact

The Air Quality Index is used by government agencies to communicate to the public how polluted the air currently is or how polluted it is forecast to become. Knowing the numerical value helps you decide whether outdoor exercise is safe.

AQI Categories Explained:

  • 0 - 50 (Good): Air quality is satisfactory and poses little or no risk.
  • 51 - 100 (Moderate): Acceptable air quality; sensitive individuals should limit prolonged outdoor exertion.
  • 101 - 200 (Unhealthy): General public and sensitive groups may experience health effects.
  • 201+ (Very Unhealthy to Hazardous): Health alert; everyone should avoid outdoor activities.
