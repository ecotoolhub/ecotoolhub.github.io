Why Standard AQI Numbers Are Not Enough

Simply knowing that the AQI is 150 or 250 doesn't help you plan your day. Our Smart AQI Planner goes beyond basic numbers by providing precise decision-making metrics for ventilation, outdoor workouts, and mask requirements based on real-time pollution thresholds.

How to Protect Yourself During Smog Seasons:

When air quality dips into unhealthy zones, microscopic pollutants (PM2.5) can enter your bloodstream through your lungs. Using air purifiers indoors, sealing doors and windows during peak pollution hours, and wearing certified N95 respirators outdoors are proven ways to safeguard your health.
