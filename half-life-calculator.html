Understanding Radioactive Decay and Half-Life Calculations

Radioactive half-life is the time required for a quantity of a radioactive isotope to reduce to half of its initial value. This principle is fundamental in nuclear chemistry, physics, radiometric dating, and medical isotope therapy.
