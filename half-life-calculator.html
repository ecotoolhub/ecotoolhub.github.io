Understanding Radioactive Decay and Half-Life Calculations

Radioactive half-life is the time required for a quantity of a radioactive isotope to reduce to half of its initial value. This principle is fundamental in nuclear chemistry, physics, radiometric dating (like carbon dating), and medical isotope therapy.

The Core Decay Formula:

The mathematical model follows exponential decay: N(t) = N₀ × (1/2)^(t / t₁/₂). Our smart calculator automates this calculation while analyzing overall isotope stability and remaining percentage instantly.
