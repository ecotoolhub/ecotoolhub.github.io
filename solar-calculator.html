How to Calculate Your Home Solar Panel & Battery Needs

Transitioning to solar energy is one of the best investments for reducing electricity bills and achieving energy independence. However, correctly sizing your system is crucial to ensure your appliances run smoothly day and night.

Understanding the Components:

  • Solar Panel Capacity (Watts): Calculated based on your daily kilowatt-hour (kWh) usage divided by peak sun hours, factoring in system efficiency losses.
  • Battery Bank Capacity (Amp-hours - Ah): Required to store surplus energy for nighttime use or cloudy days, typically calculated for a 12V system standard.

Tips for Maximum Solar Efficiency:

Always orient your solar panels toward the equator (South in the Northern Hemisphere) at an optimal tilt angle. Combining high-efficiency inverters with lithium-ion batteries will maximize your system's longevity and performance.
