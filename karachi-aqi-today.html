🌫️ Karachi AQI: What Today's Number Means

Karachi's sea breeze usually keeps its air cleaner than Lahore's, with the AQI often sitting in the moderate range (50-150). But winter months and traffic-heavy corridors can still push readings into unhealthy territory.

What each AQI band means for you in Karachi

0–50 · Good

Enjoy outdoor activities normally. Karachi rarely sees this in winter, but monsoon-season days can get here.

51–100 · Moderate

Acceptable for most people. Sensitive groups (asthma, elderly, young children) should watch for symptoms on longer outings.

101–150 · Unhealthy for sensitive groups

Children, elderly, pregnant women, and anyone with heart/lung conditions should shorten or move outdoor exertion indoors.

151–200 · Unhealthy

Everyone may feel effects. Keep windows closed, run a HEPA purifier, wear an N95 outdoors, skip the morning jog.

201–300 · Very unhealthy

Health alert conditions. Avoid all outdoor exertion; keep children and elderly indoors as much as possible.

300+ · Hazardous

Emergency conditions. Stay indoors, seal windows, run purifiers on high. Karachi hits this during peak smog episodes.

LOCAL CONTEXT

Why Karachi's air behaves this way

The Arabian Sea breeze is Karachi's natural air purifier — it disperses vehicle and industrial emissions most of the year. Pollution spikes happen in winter (weaker sea breeze, temperature inversion), near DHA/Clifton traffic corridors, and around industrial zones like Korangi and SITE.

Karachi Air Quality FAQs

Is Karachi's air quality bad?

Moderate on most days — better than Lahore thanks to the sea breeze. But winter inversions and traffic corridors regularly push AQI above 150, when sensitive groups should take precautions.

Why is Karachi's AQI better than Lahore's?

The sea breeze disperses pollutants year-round, and Karachi has no equivalent of Punjab's winter crop burning. Geography does most of the work — but it can't fix traffic exhaust.

Should I wear a mask in Karachi?

On days above AQI 150, yes — an N95/FFP2 mask. Below that, masks are optional for healthy adults but sensible for asthma patients and the elderly.

Do I need an air purifier in Karachi?

In winter or if you live near a busy road or industrial area, a HEPA purifier in the bedroom measurably improves sleep quality. In breezy coastal areas with open windows, it's less critical.
