Understanding Your Carbon Footprint and CO2 Emissions

A carbon footprint is the total amount of greenhouse gases (including carbon dioxide and methane) that are generated by our actions. Measuring your emissions is the first step toward living a more sustainable, eco-conscious lifestyle.

How are these emissions calculated?

Our calculator uses standard environmental conversion factors:

  • Vehicle Travel: Average petrol/diesel cars emit roughly 0.21 kg of CO2 per kilometer driven.
  • Electricity Consumption: Generating power produces CO2 (approx. 0.85 kg CO2 per kWh depending on the grid).
  • Gas / LPG Usage: Burning household gas releases direct carbon emissions (approx. 2.98 kg CO2 per kg).

How can you reduce your carbon footprint?

Switching to energy-efficient appliances, carpooling or using public transport, reducing single-use plastics, and adopting renewable energy sources like solar panels can dramatically lower your annual environmental impact.
