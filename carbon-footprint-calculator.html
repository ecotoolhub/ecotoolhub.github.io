Why Tracking Household Carbon Footprint Matters

Household energy consumption and transportation generate a substantial share of daily greenhouse emissions. Monitoring electricity usage, switching to efficient devices, and utilizing rainwater harvesters and portable purifiers helps minimize personal ecological impact.
