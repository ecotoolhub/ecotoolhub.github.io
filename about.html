🌱 About Eco Tool Hub

Eco Tool Hub is a free online platform offering interactive calculators and practical guides to help you live more sustainably. Our mission is simple: make green living easy, measurable, and accessible to everyone.

What We Offer

  • ☀️ Solar & Battery Sizing — calculate solar panel wattage, inverter size, and battery backup for your home.
  • 🌱 Plant Water Schedule — find the perfect watering routine for your indoor plants.
  • 🍂 Compost Mix Ratio — get the ideal carbon-to-nitrogen ratio for odor-free composting.
  • 🚗 Carbon Footprint Planner — estimate your household emissions and learn how to reduce them.
  • 💨 Air Quality Safety Evaluator — check AQI-based health guidance for your indoor space.

Why We Built This

Sustainability advice is everywhere, but practical, personalized numbers are hard to find. Our calculators turn general tips into exact figures for your home, your plants, and your lifestyle — no sign-up, no cost, no hassle.

100% Free, Forever

Every tool and guide on Eco Tool Hub is completely free to use. We keep the lights on through non-intrusive advertising, so you never have to pay or create an account.
