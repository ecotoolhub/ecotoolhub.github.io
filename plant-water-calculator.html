Mastering Indoor Plant Hydration and Humidity

Overwatering is the #1 killer of houseplants. Determining the exact volume of water and frequency based on your pot size and room climate ensures your greenery thrives without root rot or dehydration.

The Finger Test Rule:

Always check the top 2 inches of soil before watering. If it feels dry, it is time to hydrate. Pair your routine with reliable moisture meters for professional-grade indoor gardening results.
