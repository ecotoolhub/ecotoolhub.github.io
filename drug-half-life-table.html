How to Read This Drug Half-Life Chart

Half-life is the time for a drug's concentration in the blood to drop by half. After about 5 half-lives (~97% eliminated), a drug is considered cleared from the system. The "cleared in" column uses the upper end of each range to stay conservative.

Ranges exist because half-life depends on the person: age, liver and kidney function, dose, and interacting drugs all shift it. Values below are widely published clinical averages.

Drug Half-Life FAQs

How long does ibuprofen stay in your system?

About 2-hour half-life → ~97% cleared in roughly 10 hours. Pain relief typically lasts 4–6 hours per dose.

How long does Adderall stay in your system?

9–14 hour half-life → 2–3 days for near-complete clearance, depending on formulation and individual metabolism.

How long does Xanax stay in your system?

6–27 hour half-life (average ~11 h) → about 2–6 days to clear. Effects last 4–6 hours per dose.

How long does Zoloft stay in your system?

24–26 hour half-life, plus an active metabolite lasting 62–104 hours → roughly 5–6 days for near-complete clearance.

How many half-lives does it take to clear a drug?

Five. After 5 half-lives ~97% is eliminated — the standard clinical rule for "cleared from the system."
